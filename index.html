About

I am an MSc Economics student at the University of Bonn and a Research Assistant at the Institute for Macroeconomics and Econometrics. Before moving to Bonn, I received my B.S. in Economics from Universidad San Francisco de Quito, where I graduated Magna Cum Laude.

My research interests are in applied macroeconomics, with a focus on production networks, firm dynamics, and income inequality. I am particularly interested in how firms respond to economic shocks and how these shocks travel through markets, supply chains, and broader production networks.

I enjoy working on projects that combine administrative data, applied econometrics, and computational tools. More broadly, I like research questions where detailed empirical work can help us better understand economic adjustment in emerging economies.
