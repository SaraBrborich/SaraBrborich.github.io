Outside Research

I enjoy music, sports, photography, and stories that make the world feel more layered. I like spaces that invite observation: concerts, football stadiums, museums, hikes, city streets, and small everyday moments that can become beautiful when seen from the right angle.

Music and Concerts

Music is a big part of my life. I enjoy nu and symphonic metal, and my favorite band is Epica. I also love Adele, whose music I never get tired of listening to. Concerts are among my favorite places: loud, emotional, and full of energy.

Sports and Outdoors

Football is my favorite sport. I support Liga de Quitoem> in Ecuador and Real Madridem> in Spain. I also enjoy staying active through exercise, hiking, and spending time outdoors. Movement helps me clear my mind and return to work with better energy.

Photography and Places

Photography is one of the hobbies I enjoy the most. I like capturing landscapes, animals, nature, and moments that convey freedom, calm, or movement. I am drawn to simple scenes that might otherwise go unnoticed, but that can feel beautiful when seen from the right angle.

Stories, Mysteries, and Galaxies

I am drawn to stories where observation matters: clues, hidden motives, careful reasoning, and good detective work. That is probably why I enjoy detective and crime shows such as The Closer, The Mentalist, Castle, NCIS, and CSI. The Closer is my favorite TV series, and my dog, Fritzi, is named after one of its characters.

I am also a Star Wars fan. I like stories that combine mystery, strategy, politics, and world-building, and few fictional universes do that as vividly. Whether in a crime investigation or in a galaxy far, far away, I enjoy narratives that reward attention to detail and make their worlds feel larger than what is immediately on screen.
