Outside Research

I enjoy music, sports, photography, and stories that make the world feel more layered. I like spaces that invite observation: concerts, football stadiums, museums, hikes, city streets, and small everyday moments that can become beautiful when seen from the right angle.

Music and Concerts

Music is a big part of my life. I enjoy nu and symphonic metal, and my favorite band is Epica. I also love Adele, whose music I never get tired of listening to. Concerts are among my favorite places: loud, emotional, and full of energy.

Sports and Outdoors

Football is my favorite sport. I support Liga de Quito in Ecuador and Real Madrid in Spain. I also enjoy staying active through exercise, hiking, and spending time outdoors. Movement helps me clear my mind and return to work with better energy.

Photography and Places

Photography is one of the hobbies I enjoy the most. I like capturing landscapes, animals, nature, and moments that convey freedom, calm, or movement. I am drawn to simple scenes that might otherwise go unnoticed, but that can feel beautiful when seen from the right angle.

Stories, Mysteries, and Galaxies

I love stories where observation matters: clues, hidden motives, careful reasoning, and good detective work. That is probably why I enjoy shows like The Closer, The Mentalist, Criminal Minds, Castle, NCIS, and CSI. The Closer is my favorite TV series, and my dog, Fritzi, is named after one of its characters.

I am also a Star Wars fan, especially of the older Expanded Universe stories now grouped under Legends. My favorite part of that universe is Timothy Zahn's Heir to the Empire trilogy: I like its mix of strategy, politics, mystery, and world-building, and the way it makes the galaxy feel bigger, more layered, and full of unexpected connections.
