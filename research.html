Research

Before joining the University of Bonn, I worked for two years as a Research Assistant in Ecuador on projects related to trade policy, firm-level adjustment, market structure, and public policy evaluation. I also worked as an External Consultant for the Inter-American Development Bank.

I currently work as a Research Assistant at the Institute for Macroeconomics and Econometrics at the University of Bonn on the project “Growing like Korea,” supervised by Joachim Jungherr and Chi Hyun Kim.
